- Member and Graduate of the Australian Institute of Company Directors (GAICD)
- Fellow of the Australian Institute of Health and Safety (FAIHS)
- Certified Chartered OHS Professional (ChOHSP)
- Bachelor of Environmental Toxicology
- Masters of Business Administration (Accounting)
- Graduate Diploma of Occupational Health & Safety Management

Nathan is a former President of the International Network of Safety & Health Professional Organisations (INSHPO) and a former Director and Deputy Chairman of the AIHS Board. He is also a former Chair of their Finance, Risk, Audit, Performance, and Compliance Committee and a past Chair of the AIHS College of Fellows. Nathan is also a former Treasurer and Secretary for the Fremantle City RSL Sub-Branch.

With a career spanning more than 25 years, Nathan Winter has held roles as a Director and senior management executive in Health & Safety, Environment, Risk and Quality, across the Manufacturing, Mining, Oil and Gas, Services and Utilities industries.

Throughout his career, Nathan has actively contributed to the advancement of health and safety through his leadership, coaching, and development of teams of Safety, Risk, Audit, and Quality professionals. Nathan is a strong believer in collaboration and consultation both within his team, throughout organisations, and with the wider community.

Nathan combines his qualifications in Environmental Toxicology with Business Administration, Accounting, and OH&S Management. He applies his significant experience and leadership skills in delivering sustainable improvements in Risk Management, which has resulted in a higher standard of Health & Safety capability and performance amongst health and safety professionals.

A natural and effective communicator, Nathan is frequently invited to speak at major conferences, think tanks, and international congresses. Nathan’s strong organizational development and communication skills are of benefit when developing supportive relationships with regulators, Corporate partners, and counterpart organisations with whom he interacts around the world.
