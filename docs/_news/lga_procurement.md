# LGA SA Approved Supplier

Nathan Winter & Associates are pleased to announce that we are now a Local Government Association(LGA) South Australia Procurement Approved Supplier.

If you are an LGA SA Workers Compensation Scheme Member, you can now access the LGA Procurement rates for Work Health and Safety, Environmental or Quality Consulting services provided by Nathan Winter & Associates.

Simply log in to UniMarket (VendorPanel) and search for Nathan Winter & Associates in the 'Engineering & Professional Technical Consultancy Services' panel to find our Approved Supplier Agreement No: LGAP 20073, if you require any Work Health and Safety, Environmental, or Quality Consulting services.  

Please contact us at enquiries@nwassociates.com.au if you require assistance or would like any further information.
