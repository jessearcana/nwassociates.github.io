# LGA SA Approved Supplier

Nathan Winter & Associates are pleased to annouce we are now a Local Government Associaton(LGA) South Australia Procurement Approved Supplier.

If you a LGA SA Workers Compensation Scheme Member, you can now access the LGA Procurement rates for Work Health and Safety, Environmental or Quality Consulting services provided by Nathan Winter & Associates.

Simply log in to UniMarket (VendorPanel) and search for Nathan Winter & Associates in the Engineering & Professional Technical Consultancy Services Panel to find our Approved Supplier Agreement No: LGAP 20073, if you required any Work Health and Safety, Environmental or Quality Consulting services.  

Please contact us at enquiries@nwassociates.com.au if you require assistance or would like any further information.
